Scope Reality

What the internal build still has to solve

These are not features to add later. They are foundational requirements that determine whether the system is safe to run on Nexeo's website with real buyers.

01
CRM and GTM Integration
The agent has to understand leads, accounts, intent signals, qualification criteria, routing rules, rep calendars, website behavior, and email, while keeping all of it synchronized with Salesforce in real time.
Qualified is Salesforce-native. No custom integration work required.
02
Security and Governance
Nexeo would own data privacy, access controls, prompt and agent guardrails, monitoring, incident response, model change management, and ongoing compliance governance. That is a full-time responsibility before a single playbook is written.
Qualified undergoes an annual SOC 2 Type II audit, encrypts customer data, and has a formal AI Trust Framework covering privacy and governance.
03
Agent Accuracy and Control
Production AI agents require configurable goals and guardrails, response review, conversation coaching, and visibility into how decisions are made. All of that has to be designed, built, tested, and maintained internally.
Qualified provides all of this out of the box with Piper's built-in control layer.
04
Ongoing Optimization
AI is not finished at launch. Knowledge changes, prompts need refinement, models evolve, and edge cases emerge. Industry estimates put maintenance at 15% to 25% of initial development cost annually, plus LLM inference and cloud infrastructure.
Qualified's Success Architect owns this work on Nexeo's behalf as part of the subscription.
05
Feature Parity
Even the expanded build model does not reach full Piper parity. Piper already operates across the website and inbox with qualification, routing, meeting booking, workflows, analytics, enrichment, text, voice, and video capabilities. Each of those is a separate build workstream.
All capabilities are live and included in Nexeo's contract from day one.

Execution Risk

Three risks that do not show up in the build estimate

These are independent third-party data points. They are not Salesforce projections.

01
Gartner
Budget and delivery risk
Gartner says at least 50% of GenAI projects will exceed budget through 2028 because of architecture and operational challenges. More than 40% of agentic AI projects are predicted to be canceled by end of 2027 because of cost, unclear business value, or inadequate risk controls.
02
MIT NANDA via Fortune
Build success rate
Preliminary MIT NANDA research reported by Fortune found purchased AI solutions succeeded more often than internal builds. Treat this as directional, not definitive. It reinforces the execution risk of building and maintaining an internal AI product rather than deploying a purpose-built platform.
03
Air Canada / ABA
Liability
Companies remain responsible for what customer-facing AI says. In the Air Canada case, the BC Civil Resolution Tribunal held the company liable for inaccurate information from its website chatbot. Buying software does not eliminate this responsibility, but mature guardrails, monitoring, and governance reduce exposure. Qualified's platform includes all three.
Not Included in the Build Estimates
Voice, video, recruiting time, security audits, major compliance work, internal management overhead, and the opportunity cost of pulling engineers away from Nexeo's core product roadmap are not included in the build figures above. Including any of these would push year one costs materially higher.
Executive Takeaway

Nexeo can build this.
The real question is whether it should become one of Nexeo's software products.

Qualified deploys in weeks instead of months, transfers the technical and operational burden to a vendor whose entire business is building this capability, and gives Nexeo a platform that keeps evolving without requiring an internal team to rebuild every new feature.
