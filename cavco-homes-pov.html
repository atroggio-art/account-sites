The Opportunity

Cavco has built a remarkable company. The next phase is about building the system around it.

You have 20-plus home brands. You have a dealer network that most competitors spend decades trying to build. You have manufacturing capacity spread across the country, and a HUD-code product that is genuinely the most affordable path to homeownership in America.

What you do not have yet is a unified view of the buyer journey from the moment someone searches for a home online to the moment they get the keys. That gap costs you leads, creates friction for dealers, and makes it nearly impossible to understand which markets and which buyers are most valuable to pursue.

"The best home builders will not win on product alone. They will win on who knows their buyer best."

That is the window this brief is about. Not technology for its own sake. A connected system that makes your dealers more effective, your marketing more accountable, and your operations faster to respond when a buyer raises their hand.

Why Now

Three reasons the window
to act is right now, not next quarter.

Clayton and Champion are investing in digital buyer experience right now

Both competitors have active CRM and marketing automation initiatives underway. Clayton in particular has made buyer data infrastructure a stated strategic priority. Every month that passes without a comparable Cavco capability is a month of leads going to a competitor who is faster to respond and better at follow-up.

Dealer churn is expensive and it is driven by lead quality, not product quality

When dealers defect to competitors, they rarely cite the home itself. They cite the lead flow, the follow-up tools, and the support they receive. Fragmented systems mean Cavco cannot see where leads are dying in the dealer pipeline, which means the problem compounds silently. A connected dealer platform changes this equation directly.

A focused pilot can show results in 90 days, not 18 months

This does not need to be an enterprise transformation on day one. A scoped engagement with one brand, one region, and two or three dealer partners can demonstrate measurable improvement in lead response time and deal velocity within a single quarter. That result becomes the business case for the broader rollout, and it gives internal champions something concrete to show leadership.
