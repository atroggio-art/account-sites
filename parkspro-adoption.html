The ParksPro Playbook

Measure 3 things. Only 3.

Everything else is noise. If every rep knows these three numbers cold, the whole system works.

01
KPI 1
Activities
  • Face-to-face visits
  • Phone and virtual calls
  • Every touch logged in Salesforce, same day
  • Target: 8 face-to-face per week per rep
02
KPI 2
Pipeline
  • Next step on every open deal, no exceptions
  • Accurate close date, amount, and stage
  • Zero zombie deals, zero stalled opps
  • Velocity: deals moving or deals closing
03
KPI 3
Quotes
  • How many customers did you quote this week?
  • How many this month?
  • Quote follow-up completed: 100%
  • Every quote tracked in Salesforce, not email

Execution Plan

Six steps. Ten weeks.

Each step builds on the last. The goal is not a Salesforce rollout. It is a sales operating rhythm ParksPro actually runs on.

1
Step 01
Complete
Define the ParksPro Standard
Austin session complete. Reverse demo done. Leadership aligned on what behavior looks like and what success means before any rep gets a scorecard.
  • Calls, visits, and quotes per week per rep confirmed
  • Touch every account every 90 days (stated goal)
  • Pipeline hygiene rules: stage, close date, next step
  • Quote follow-up window defined
2
Step 02
In Progress
Simplify the Experience
Remove friction from every screen a rep touches. If it does not matter to Fleet Commercial, it is not on the page.
  • Trim Account, Opportunity, and Quote/BOS to what gets used
  • Fix open Phase 1 gaps: CSV bucket-list upload, Opp with Stock/VIN, BOS edits after creation
  • Document the standard Quoting flow and BOS flow
3
Step 03
In Progress
Set Expectations and Guardrails
Publish the ParksPro Standard. Every rep and every manager knows the number. No ambiguity about what good looks like.
  • Expectations published in writing to all reps
  • Guardrails enforced in Salesforce where possible
  • Managers coached on how to hold the standard
4
Step 04
Launching Week 1
Weekly Leadership Scorecard
Every week, pull Salesforce reports and use Claude to build a 4-slide executive summary. Leaders get the story, not a dashboard to dig through.
  • Score every team and every seller
  • Call out reps improving, not just reps struggling
  • A 120-seller CRO review that used to take 3 days now takes about 4 minutes with CoWorker
  • Prompts built into a dropdown so leaders run them in one click
5
Step 05
Launching Week 1
Weekly Office Hours
Standing weekly slot. 10-minute focused topic, then open Q&A. 15 to 30 sellers attending. Not a training. A working session.
  • Logging activities and creating opportunities
  • Opp with Stock number or VIN
  • Quoting flow and BOS edits
  • Mobile prospect upload and reading the dashboard
  • Using CoWorker prompts in the field
6
Step 06
Weeks 2-8
Field and Mobile
Reps drive 800 miles a week. The CRM has to work in the car, not just at a desk. Voice-to-text closes the debrief gap without adding time.
  • Voice-to-text for call debriefs and CRM updates on the road
  • Prospect upload on mobile (already deployed)
  • Enablement sessions at dealerships weeks 5 to 8
