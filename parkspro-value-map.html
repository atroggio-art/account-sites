Adoption and Accountability
ROI Depends on Consistent Use — Not Just Access
The deck is explicit: adoption is part of the operating system. ROI only materializes when the sales organization uses the workflow consistently.
1
Required Workflow
Sales activity, opportunities, and quotes live in Salesforce — not in spreadsheets or separate tools.
2
Minimum Expectations
Define the activities and data required from every salesperson. No ambiguity about what "using the system" means.
3
Manager Scorecards
Managers see adoption, pipeline, and follow-up in one view. Performance is visible before it becomes a problem.
4
Weekly Pipeline Review
Accountability meetings run from Salesforce data — not anecdote, spreadsheets, or memory.
5
Training and Coaching
Train the process, reinforce it, and address gaps quickly. Salesforce is the workflow, not a supplement to it.
6
Controlled Change
System issues and requests follow a defined governance process. The system improves continuously, not by individual workaround.
