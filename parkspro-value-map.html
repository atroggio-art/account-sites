Company Context
Business model profile and key inputs used

ParksPro is the commercial fleet and leasing division of Parks Automotive Group, a $300M+ operation founded in 1962. In just 18 months the division built a $30M leasing business from zero, marking a deliberate go-to-market shift from transactional fleet sales to recurring-revenue leasing.

The Neighborly contract — 28,000 vehicles going live in July, projected to reach $140M by Year 3 — is the forcing function pushing ParksPro to fix its operational backbone at scale. The long-term ambition is a $1B+ national brand, which requires a repeatable operating model across markets rather than a single-store way of working.
