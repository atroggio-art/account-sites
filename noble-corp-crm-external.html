Where Noble Leaves Revenue on the Table
Three Gaps. One Platform to Close Them.
Offshore drilling is a relationship business — but relationships don't scale without infrastructure. Here's where Noble's commercial motion breaks down today.
01
Operator Engagement
Key accounts go dark between contract cycles.
Relationships with oil majors — Shell, BP, TotalEnergies, Petrobras — are maintained person-to-person, not systematically. When a contract ends, so does the outreach. By the next tendering cycle, Noble is one of five names in the RFQ with no differentiated presence.
02
Talent Pipeline
Offshore talent is scarce and the pipeline is manual.
Recruiting drillers, toolpushers, and offshore engineers in a constrained market requires long-term nurturing — not reactive job posts. Noble has no automated talent journey to keep passive candidates warm across the 6-18 month hiring cycle.
03
Marketing Visibility
No line of sight from content to contract value.
Digital touchpoints generate no actionable signal. Marketing can't prove ROI or identify which operators are entering an active tendering cycle before the RFQ goes public — leaving sales to work cold instead of warm.
