Problem Statement

AWG's quoting, pricing, and contract management processes are highly manual, fragmented, and dependent on tribal knowledge. Reps negotiate pricing independently with no guardrails, contracts are scattered across email and shared drives, and finance has no real-time view into committed revenue. This makes it difficult for AWG to protect margin, accelerate deal cycles, or produce accurate forecasts — and the problem compounds as commercial volume grows.

Objective

Standardize and automate AWG's quote-to-cash motion — from guided selling and pricing governance through contract lifecycle management and revenue recognition — using Revenue Cloud Advanced.

Provide AWG with a connected commercial platform that protects margin at every deal, accelerates time to close, and gives leadership real-time visibility from pipeline to booked revenue.

Key Outcomes
  • ✓ Reps generate accurate, approved quotes in minutes — not days
  • ✓ Leadership has full control over discounting to protect margin
  • ✓ Centralized pricing rules enforced automatically at point of sale
  • ✓ Contract renewals are automated with proactive alerts and upsell signals
  • ✓ Finance sees committed revenue in real time — no more end-of-quarter surprises
  • ✓ Single connected platform from CPQ through billing and revenue recognition
