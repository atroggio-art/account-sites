What Is a Blueprint?
The blueprint is the unit of work. One blueprint = one process. ParksPro starts with Opportunity to Order Management and adds blueprints as processes are validated.
Definition
  • A blueprint is a process that has a clear start and end — structured, repeatable, and assigned to the right agent or person at each stage.
  • A blueprint can contain linked blueprints (sub-processes). Linked blueprints cannot run as standalone processes — they are components of the master workflow.
Variations
  • A blueprint can have variants based on different geographies, products, or customer types — Neighborly vs. standard fleet orders, for example.
  • A blueprint has unlimited versions as the process changes over time — no IT bottleneck to update the workflow as ParksPro operations evolve.
