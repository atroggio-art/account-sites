Company Context
ParksPro Upfitting Division — Business Model Profile
The Division
The Upfitting Division is the first operational step after a vehicle is sourced. It takes a finalized order and turns it into a physically completed, delivery-ready vehicle: configuration, parts staging, bay scheduling, upfit work, graphics, inspection, rework if needed, and release.
The Forcing Function
The Neighborly contract (28,000 vehicles, ramping to $140M by Year 3) is straining a fulfillment model built for one-truck orders. The division was not designed for national-account volume — and the manual coordination layer is the constraint.
Why This Is Urgent
Vertically integrated shop economics mean efficiency gains flow straight to divisional profit, not to a third-party upfitter's margin. Quote turnaround time is a named, explicit pain point. The 20-person team runs intake, scheduling, and coordination manually today — spreadsheets, calls, and tribal knowledge substitute for a system of record.
The Salesforce Opportunity
Service Cloud and Agentforce for Service address both the revenue side (faster quoting wins time-sensitive fleet business) and the cost side (automated coordination frees capacity, reduces rework). Together they provide the single operating picture the COO needs to manage delivery reliability at Neighborly scale.
