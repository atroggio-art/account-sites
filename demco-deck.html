Current State
Three Problems. One Root Cause.

Every symptom Demco described — slow quotes, data errors, dealer disengagement — traces back to the same thing: a commercial stack built inside-out, not customer-first.

01
Problem 1
Quote Speed Kills Deals
30 min average compile time. Reps won't quote live with a customer on the phone. Dealers have learned not to even call for a price because they know they'll wait four days. That's invisible lost revenue.
02
Problem 2
10% of Quotes Crash and Delete
On-prem ERP syncs to cloud CPQ once nightly. Any mid-week part number or operational change creates a mismatch. The quote compiles, fails, and wipes itself. Rep starts over from scratch.
03
Problem 3
Engineering Codes, Not Customer Language
Options are presented as concatenated engineering part strings — 17 dash-delineated digits that mean something to ops, nothing to a dealer. The Ford F-150 configurator doesn't show you the chassis specs. Demco's does.
Root Cause
The system was built to serve engineering and operations — not dealers and sales reps. Every patch since has inherited that debt. The fix isn't another patch. It's a platform built commercial-first from the ground up.
