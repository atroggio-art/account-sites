The Math on Member Retention
A 5% improvement in member retention in a subscription model translates to 25-95% improvement in profitability over time - because the acquisition cost is already spent. At Zips' scale, this is not a marketing metric - it's a revenue line item.
What We're Proposing
Four Capabilities That Close the Gaps
Each one maps to a specific break in the member lifecycle. Together, they turn Zips' existing Data Cloud and MC investment into a full-stack retention engine.
Stage 02, 05 — Loyalty Management
Turn Membership Into a Points-and-Perks Engine
Loyalty Management gives Zips a native program layer: tiers, points on washes, referral credits, and reward redemption - all sitting on top of Data Cloud. Members who earn points wash more frequently. Members in a tier cancel less. The structure builds the behavior that protects revenue.

Combined with Retail Cloud, Zips can manage the entire program in one place - no bolted-on third-party loyalty tool.
Stage 04 — Agentforce Churn Prevention
An AI Agent That Watches for Churn Before It Happens
Agentforce for Membership Churn Prevention monitors Data Cloud signals in real time - visit frequency drops, payment failures, location changes, weather patterns - and triggers personalized outreach the moment a member shows at-risk behavior.

Not a batch report. Not a weekly campaign. A live agent that acts on the signal the day it surfaces - before the customer makes the decision to cancel.
Stage 02, 03 — Journey Automation
A Message for Every Moment in the Member Lifecycle
Zips has MC Engagement live and SMS running. The gap is the journey logic that connects them. Journey Builder across the lifecycle means a new trial member gets an onboarding flow. An active member gets a tier-up nudge at 10 washes. A member who skips two weeks gets a weather-triggered re-engagement offer.

All of this runs on the infrastructure Zips already owns. The playbook just needs to be built.
Stage 03, 04 — Conversational Channels
Meet Members in WhatsApp and RCS, Not Just SMS
Zips is running SMS short code today. That covers a lot of ground - but younger members are more responsive on WhatsApp and RCS, where rich cards, carousels, and two-way conversation happen natively.

The loyalty offer that gets read is the one that gets acted on. Adding conversational channels to the mix ensures Zips' messages land where the member actually spends time.

The programs take time to build - starting now puts Zips in position to capture the season.
