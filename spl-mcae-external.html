The Market Has Changed
Three Realities SPL's Marketing Stack Must Answer
Market Reality 01
13 buyers. 2 departments. One deal.
The buying group for industrial services contracts now averages 13 people across 2 departments. Email to a single contact isn't a nurture strategy — it's a coin flip on whether the right person sees it.
Market Reality 02
68% of buyers have a front-runner before sales calls.
Self-directed research via LLMs and digital channels means buyers arrive pre-decided. The companies winning are the ones engaged during that research phase — not after it.
Market Reality 03
Traditional signals are going dark.
Zero-click research, private browsing, and LLM-mediated discovery mean web visits and form fills no longer capture the full picture. Only 48% of marketers have fully integrated cross-functional data to compensate.
