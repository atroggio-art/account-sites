Fixing the
Revenue Engine

Where friction is costing AWG today, and the path to a connected, scalable quote-to-cash motion.

AWG operates a complex, high-volume B2B commercial model, managing pricing, contracts, and revenue across a large network of members, dealers, and buyers. Like most organizations at this scale, revenue operations have grown faster than the systems supporting them. The result is friction, cost, and missed opportunity at every stage of the commercial cycle.
The Problem

Five places the current model breaks down

Surfaced across discovery conversations with AWG's commercial and finance leadership.

1

Quoting is slow and error-prone

Reps build quotes manually through spreadsheets and back-and-forth with finance. Deals that should close in days take weeks. Pricing errors erode margin and damage buyer trust.

2

Pricing complexity is unmanageable

Volume tiers, contract pricing, rebates, promotional adjustments. No single system enforces the rules. Every deal becomes a one-off negotiation with no guardrails.

3

Revenue visibility is a black box

Finance cannot see what has been committed in the field until it hits the books. Forecasting is reactive. Revenue recognition is manual and audit-heavy.

4

Contract management is scattered

Contracts live in email, shared drives, and individual reps' desks. Renewals get missed. Terms drift. There is no proactive view of what is expiring or what is at risk.

5

Systems don't talk to each other

Sales, finance, and operations each run their own tools. Constant re-entry. The handoff from closed-won to billing to fulfillment breaks down repeatedly, creating downstream customer experience problems.

Why Now

Three reasons waiting costs more than moving

Driver 01

Complexity is accelerating, not plateauing

AWG's commercial model is getting more sophisticated. Manual processes that barely work today will break under tomorrow's volume and velocity.

Driver 02

Buyers expect fast, accurate responses

Decision cycles are compressing. Buyers who wait days for a quote are already talking to someone else. Speed is now a competitive differentiator.

Driver 03

ASC 606 compliance pressure is real

Finance and audit scrutiny around revenue recognition continues to increase. Manual recognition processes are a liability, not just an inconvenience.

The result: a revenue engine that runs itself

A connected quote-to-cash motion that shortens deal cycles, protects margin, gives finance real-time revenue visibility, and lets AWG's commercial team focus on selling rather than fixing spreadsheets.
