03 · The 6-Step 30-Day Journey

From picking a champion to a live demo for the decision maker

Six steps, four weeks. Each step has a clear owner, clear inputs, and a clear output.

1
Step 1 Week 1
Identify the customer & champion

Pick the account and the internal champion you will build the agent with. The champion carries the message, runs the demo, and owns adoption after you leave.

  • Choose a customer motivated to drive Salesforce adoption and visibility
  • Find a champion who is respected, hands-on in CRM, and wants to lead the AI story
  • Confirm the champion has access to build and demo in their own org
2
Step 2 Week 1
First call: win stories & the why

Run the kickoff. Show win stories and align on one solid POC goal, then prove it. Data entry is a high-value use case; stay inside Salesforce to avoid flex credits.

  • Open with win stories that mirror the customer's business
  • Align on one solid POC goal and prove that success
  • Lead with data entry as a high-value use case
  • Stay inside Salesforce to avoid flex credits and get easy quick wins
3
Step 3 Week 2
Identify end users

Segment end users into power and struggling users. Power users become multipliers; struggling users become the adoption proof point.

  • List power users, fast adopters who model the behavior
  • List struggling users, low CRM usage, manual workflows
  • Plan a help path for each segment
4
Step 4 Week 2
One-on-one interviews

Sit with each end user. Teach them how to use Agentforce led by the Agentforce AE, and create 1 to 3 skills with them.

  • Teach each user how to use Agentforce, led by the Agentforce AE
  • Create 1 to 3 skills together with each user
  • Map each user's real workflow and the manual, avoided tasks
5
Step 5 Week 3
Deliverables: roadmap & value summary

Produce a 10 use case roadmap of low-complexity skills. Build the Business Value Summary and executive value map with Slackbot, plus the sheet quantifying data-entry impact.

  • 10 use case roadmap, skills and agents, mostly low complexity
  • Executive value map built with Slackbot
  • Value sheet quantifying data-entry and streamlining impact
6
Step 6 Week 4
Champion demos to the decision maker

The champion demos Coworker live in their own org and shows the results, proof of a successful AI strategy delivered inside 30 days.

  • Champion leads the demo in their real org
  • Show results: activity logged, time saved, CRM completeness
  • Make the ask to expand and commit

06 · Champion & First Call

What makes a good champion

The champion is the most important variable in the 30 days. They carry the message, run the demo, and own adoption after you leave.

Respected by peers and credible with the decision maker

Hands-on in Salesforce every day, feels the data-entry pain

Wants to own and lead the AI story, not just sponsor it

Able to build and demo in their own org

Motivated by adoption and visibility, not just the technology itself

First-Call Agenda: Challenge the Why

Customers want more from their Salesforce investment, and most have not seen a successful AI investment turn into value. That is the opening.

Win stories to show

  • Sellers logging activity by voice instead of typing after every call
  • Pipeline dashboards that are finally complete and trusted
  • Struggling reps brought up to the standard of power users
  • Hours of admin time returned to selling each week
Challenge the Why: Discovery Questions
  • Why do this now, and what breaks if nothing changes?
  • What does success actually look like in 30 days?
  • Where is Salesforce data incomplete today, and what does that cost in visibility?
  • Who feels the pain most, and who benefits most from fixing it?
  • If adoption goes up, what decisions get better?

Align and prove success. Data entry is a high-value use case. Stay inside Salesforce to avoid flex credits and get easy quick wins.

08 · End Users & Interviews

End user segmentation

Segment end users into power and struggling users. Power users become multipliers; struggling users become the adoption proof point for the decision maker.

Segment What they need How Coworker helps Interview prompts
Power users Speed, fewer clicks, and advanced moves that compound their edge Coworker removes admin drag: voice-to-CRM, next-step summaries, prospecting. They sell more and model the behavior. What slows you down in CRM? Which repetitive task would you hand off first? Where could an agent save you an hour a day?
Struggling users Lower friction and confidence, a reason to actually use Salesforce Coworker logs activity for them and drafts the work, turning CRM from a chore into a helper. They become the clearest adoption proof point. What keeps you out of Salesforce? What do you track outside CRM today? If updates happened automatically, what would change?

13 · Common Blockers & Mitigations

Protect the 30 days

Every program faces these four blockers. Anticipate them at kickoff and the Week 4 demo stays on track.

1

Provisioning delays

Confirm Agentforce and Data Foundations provisioning before Week 1. Escalate early so the Week 4 demo is never at risk.

2

Data readiness

Assess CRM data quality up front. Scope low-complexity use cases that work with data as-is. Don't let perfect be the enemy of proven.

3

Change management

Use the struggling-user segment and champion-led demos to build belief. Show, don't tell. Peer-to-peer proof converts faster than vendor pitches.

4

Champion availability

Secure the champion's time for all four weeks at kickoff. Name a backup contact. The champion going dark is the single biggest program killer.
